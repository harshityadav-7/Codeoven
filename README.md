# 🔥 CodeOven
Competitive programming tracker • Developer leaderboard • Coding activity analytics
**The Fitness App for Competitive Programmers** — CodeOven is a unified data aggregation and motivation ecosystem designed for the modern developer. It synchronizes fragmented coding activity from across the web into a single, high-performance dashboard—empowering engineers to track progress, visualize growth, and compete at a global scale.

## 🚀 Key Modules

### 🛠️ The Forge (Daily Practice)
**Stop guessing. Start improving.**  
The Forge analyzes your historical performance to generate three targeted problems every 24 hours. Focused precisely on your weakest topics, it ensures no skill gap goes unfilled. Build your streak, earn freezes, and conquer the crucible.

### 🏟️ Contest Arena
**Your global competitive calendar.**  
A centralized hub tracking upcoming contests across LeetCode, Codeforces, CodeChef, and AtCoder. Pin your upcoming battles to your dashboard with real-time countdown timers.

### 📊 S-Score Engine
**One Score. Total Clarity.**  
Our proprietary S-Score algorithm calculates a unified proficiency rating by aggregating problem difficulty, quantity, and contest ratings across all linked platforms. Competitive programming, finally quantified.

### 🥇 Rank Tiers & Identity
**Apprentice → Challenger → Knight → Master → Elite Grandmaster**  
Earn your place among the elite. Progress through dynamically calculated rank tiers and unlock unique badges that showcase your expertise on your public profile.

## 🛡️ Security & Integrity

- **Platform Verification:** Unique token-based ownership verification for all linked accounts.
